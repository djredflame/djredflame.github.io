About Me

Passionate about technology, electronic music and creative digital experiences. What started as curiosity became two major passions in my life: software development and music production.

I started producing music at the age of 12, experimenting with FL Studio, later moving to Reason and eventually working with tools like Ableton Live and Korg Gadget. Electronic Dance Music, House and Electro became the foundation of my creative style and artistic identity.

My journey into software development began a few years later through self-learning and curiosity for how applications, games and digital systems work. Starting with small projects and ActionScript, I continued learning through Unity, C#, full-stack web development and modern frontend technologies.

Today, I mainly work with Angular and modern full-stack technologies while continuously expanding my knowledge across software architecture, backend development, embedded systems and creative technologies.

This portfolio combines both worlds, music and software engineering, representing the connection between creativity, technology and digital experiences.
